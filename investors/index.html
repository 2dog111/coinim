A paper letter to the investors who fit your company.

Our closed base holds 1,000,000 entrepreneurs with capital from $1,000,000 who are inclined to invest. It is not published and there is no list to browse. We select from it for one company at a time and send paper letters. The way in is a Market Scan.

Send your website What a Market Scan is

An inquiry, not an order. Nothing is sent to investors until you decide.

How it goes.

You send your website. We run a Market Scan: what you sell, who buys it, what an investor would need to see to read past the first line. Every coin.im campaign starts with it.

From the base we draw a selection for your company: entrepreneurs whose capital, sector and geography fit your round. You see who is in the selection and why before anything is written.

If you want the letters sent, we write one letter around your strongest supported facts, print it and deliver it by courier to a business address. If you do not, the Scan stays yours and nothing is sent.

Who is in the base

Entrepreneurs with capital from $1,000,000 who are inclined to invest, documented from public professional information. The base is closed: names are never published, and a selection is shown only to the company it was made for. A selection reflects fit against your criteria, not a prediction of interest.

To request removal from the base, write to mail@coin.im.
