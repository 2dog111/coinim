# Chat-first references — 2026-11-01

Goal: replace the homepage setup form with one natural-language entry point while preserving coin.im's deterministic calculations and explicit constraints.

The complete reference source is [dim.im/i](https://dim.im/i). Its reusable `task-input` technique says to ask for the minimum useful material, show examples beside the input, and reveal optional controls progressively.

## Chosen direction

Primary references: Perplexity + Kimi + Z.ai.

- One large composer is the only setup control on the first screen.
- The prompt explains that the visitor may include coin, budget, acceptable loss, and exit timing in one message.
- Three example prompts demonstrate complete, incomplete, and comparison-oriented questions.
- After submission, the same conversation shows extracted plan facts as compact chips.
- Clicking a fact prepares a natural-language change in the composer; no return to a form is required.
- Deterministic calculations, missing-data states, conflict handling, retry, stop, copy, and reset remain in the conversation workspace.
- The product examples and research stay below the first screen as evidence of output, not prerequisites for starting.

## Deliberately excluded

- A budget/loss/deadline form before the first message.
- Mode selectors, model selectors, and advanced settings on the opening screen.
- Decorative AI gradients or glass effects that compete with the input.
- Claims that the assistant has live quotes, trades funds, or enforces a stop.
